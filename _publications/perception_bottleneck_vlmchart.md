### Abstract

Vision-language models (VLMs) have shown remarkable capabilities in understanding charts, but they still struggle with complex visual reasoning tasks. We conduct a systematic analysis of the perception bottleneck in VLMs for chart understanding, identifying key architectural components that limit performance. Through extensive experiments on diverse chart types, we demonstrate that the bottleneck stems from both visual encoding and cross-modal fusion mechanisms. Our findings provide insights for designing more effective VLM architectures for chart understanding tasks.
