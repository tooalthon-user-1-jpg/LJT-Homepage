### Abstract

Parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) methods have become essential for adapting large language models to specific tasks. We investigate a novel approach that composes parameter-efficient modules using arithmetic operations, enabling efficient model adaptation with minimal parameter changes. Our method leverages the algebraic structure of arithmetic operations to create modular PEFT components that can be composed and decomposed flexibly, providing a new perspective on efficient model adaptation.
