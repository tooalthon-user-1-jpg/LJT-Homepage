We present C-Eval, a comprehensive evaluation suite for Chinese foundation models that spans multiple levels and disciplines, providing a standardized benchmark for assessing model capabilities across diverse Chinese domains.

### Abstract

The rapid development of Chinese foundation models has created an urgent need for comprehensive evaluation tools. We introduce C-Eval, a multi-level, multi-discipline evaluation suite specifically designed for Chinese foundation models. C-Eval covers a wide range of disciplines including mathematics, science, history, and more, organized across multiple difficulty levels to provide a thorough assessment of model capabilities. Our extensive evaluation demonstrates that while current models perform well on some tasks, significant challenges remain across many domains.
