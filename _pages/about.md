**PhD Candidate** — Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)

PhD Candidate at [HKUST NLP Group](https://nlp.cuhk.edu.hk/), advised by [Prof. Junxian He](https://github.com/junxianhe).

I am a first-year PhD candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://nlp.cuhk.edu.hk/), where I work on natural language processing and machine learning, with a focus on LLM reasoning, reinforcement learning for LLMs, and hallucination in vision-language models. My research interests lie at the intersection of NLP and core AI reasoning — building trustworthy and capable AI systems that can reason reliably, understand visual content accurately, and communicate truthfully.

Research Interests
======
* **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**: Exploring how to improve the reasoning capabilities of large language models through better training paradigms, evaluation, and optimization methods.
* **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**: Studying why VLMs hallucinate visual content and how to mitigate this critical issue.
* **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**: Analyzing the underlying mechanisms that govern factuality in LLMs, including how truthful representations emerge and can be controlled.
